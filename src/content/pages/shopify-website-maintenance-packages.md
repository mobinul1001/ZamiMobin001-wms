## How to Put a Shopify Store in Maintenance Mode

Shopify does not have a one-click maintenance mode like WordPress. To take your store offline temporarily, you have two options:

-   **Password protection** — in your Shopify admin, go to *Online Store > Preferences* and enable the password page to restrict access while you work.
-   **A maintenance-mode app** — install a dedicated app to show a branded "we'll be back soon" page without fully locking the store.

As part of our Shopify maintenance packages, we handle this for you whenever updates or redesigns require it. (Note: "is Shopify down for maintenance?" usually refers to Shopify's own platform status, which you can check on the official <a href="https://www.shopifystatus.com/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Shopify status page</a>.)
