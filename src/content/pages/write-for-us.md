Guest Contributions

## We Accept Relevant Website Maintenance, WordPress & Web Tech Guest Posts

Website Maintenance Services is a dedicated digital support company serving growing businesses, digital agencies, and eCommerce brands across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Our platform covers website maintenance, WordPress care, technical security, speed optimization, site migrations, web redesign, and digital strategy.

If you have genuine expertise, professional experience, research, technical knowledge, real case studies, or useful industry insights that can benefit our international audience, you are welcome to submit an article for editorial consideration.

Note: We do not automatically publish every submission. Every article is reviewed for relevance, originality, usefulness, quality, accuracy, readability, and overall suitability for our technical readership.

Editorial Guidelines

## Guest Post Submission Requirements

Every draft must satisfy these strict quality standards before our editorial team schedules it for publishing:

<div class="cards">

<div class="card">

#### 800 to 1,500 Words

Articles must be concise yet thorough, ranging between 800 and 1,500 words of actionable technical content.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### 1–2 Do-Follow Links

You may include 1 to 2 permanent, contextual Do-Follow links within the article body to your relevant website.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### AI Content Policy

AI-assisted drafts are accepted only if deeply edited with genuine human effort, real screenshots, and verified examples. Raw AI garbage is rejected.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### Relevant to Our Industry

The article must directly relate to website maintenance, WordPress, web design, security, speed, or SEO.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### Useful to Readers

Articles should educate, explain, analyze, or provide practical tutorials rather than openly advertise a commercial service.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### Reliable Sources & Citations

Technical claims, statistics, and code references should be backed by credible citations (e.g., WordPress.org, W3C, Google).

</div>

<div class="card">

#### No Keyword Stuffing

Content written to manipulate search rankings, over-optimized anchor text, or unnatural phrasing will be edited or rejected.

</div>

<div class="card">

#### Editorial Revisions

We reserve the right to edit formatting, headings, grammar, and links when necessary to protect site quality.

</div>

</div>
