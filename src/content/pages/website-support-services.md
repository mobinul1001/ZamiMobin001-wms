Overview

## What Are Website Support Services?

Website support services are ongoing technical assistance and proactive maintenance provided by a specialist team to keep your website secure, fast, and fully functional.

Unlike a one-off web design project, website support is a continuous service that handles everything from CMS updates and security patches to bug fixes, content changes, and emergency response.

**The distinction between support and maintenance is subtle but important.** Maintenance is scheduled and proactive — it happens whether or not anything is broken. Support is responsive — it's there when you need a human who knows your site and can fix something fast.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Our support team is here to help.

What is included in a website support plan?

A website support plan typically includes CMS updates (core, plugins, themes), security scanning and patching, regular backups, uptime monitoring, bug fixes, and access to a support team for technical assistance. How quickly will you respond to a support request?

Response times depend on your plan. Professional plan clients receive a 24-hour response. Priority plan subscribers receive a 4-hour response SLA, with emergency escalation available around the clock.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All of our website support plans are month-to-month. There are no setup fees or termination penalties. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

What platforms do you support?

We provide web support services for WordPress (including WooCommerce), Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, and custom-built sites on PHP or HTML/CSS stacks. If you're unsure whether your platform is covered, contact us and we'll confirm before you commit to anything.

What's the difference between website support and website maintenance?

Website maintenance refers to scheduled, proactive tasks — updates, backups, scans — that happen on a regular cycle regardless of whether something is broken. Website support adds the human, responsive layer: a team that responds to issues, answers questions, and fixes problems on demand. Our service combines both.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can change your plan at the start of any billing cycle. If your needs change — for example, you launch an ecommerce feature that requires tighter response times — we'll move you to the appropriate tier with no disruption.

What happens if my site goes down at 2am?

Priority plan clients have access to our emergency response line 24/7. Our on-call team will acknowledge your issue within the emergency SLA window and begin diagnosis immediately. For Essential and Professional clients, we offer emergency support as a paid add-on.
