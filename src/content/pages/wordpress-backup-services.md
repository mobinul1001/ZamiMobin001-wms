## Frequently Asked Questions About WordPress Backup Services

Everything you need to know about off-site cloud storage, 1-click restores, retention history, and managed website protection:

1\. What is a WordPress backup service?

A WordPress backup service automatically copies your site's files and database to a secure off-site location on a schedule, so it can be restored if something goes wrong.

2\. How often should a WordPress site be backed up?

Daily, at minimum — more frequently (hourly or real-time) for e-commerce sites where order data changes constantly.

3\. Where are backups stored?

Off-site, on cloud infrastructure separate from your hosting server, so a host outage or server failure can't affect your backup.

4\. How long does a restore take?

Most restores complete in under 30 minutes once initiated, though size and complexity can affect this.

5\. What's the difference between a backup plugin and a backup service?

A plugin runs the backup; a service monitors that it actually worked, tests restores in advance, and provides support when something breaks.

6\. Will backups slow down my website?

No — incremental backups run in the background and are scheduled to avoid peak traffic hours.

7\. What's included in a full WordPress backup?

Files, themes, plugins, uploads/media, and the full database.

8\. Can I restore just one page instead of the whole site?

Yes — granular restore lets you roll back specific files, pages, or database tables without affecting the rest of the site.

9\. What happens if my site gets hacked — can a backup fix that?

Yes, provided you have a clean restore point from before the infection, which is why pre-update and scheduled backups matter.

10\. How much does a WordPress backup service cost?

Backups are included in every plan, from $79/month. Emergency restore without a plan starts at $99.

11\. Do I need a backup service if my host already backs up my site?

Usually yes — most host backups live on the same server, meaning a server failure can take both your site and your backup down together.

12\. What is off-site backup?

Off-site backup means your data is stored on infrastructure physically and logically separate from where your live site runs.

13\. Can backups protect against ransomware?

Yes, if backups are stored off-site and immutable/version-controlled, since a clean restore point can bypass the need to pay a ransom.

14\. How long are backups kept (retention)?

30 days on Essential, 60 days on Growth, and 90 days on Business, giving you options to roll back further than just the most recent copy.

15\. Is WordPress backup necessary for a small business site?

Yes — small business sites are frequent targets for automated attacks specifically because they're less likely to have monitoring in place.

16\. What's the difference between incremental and full backups?

A full backup copies everything; an incremental backup only copies what's changed since the last backup, saving time and storage.

17\. Do you test that backups actually work?

Yes — scheduled restore testing in a staging environment confirms backups are usable before an emergency, not during one.

18\. Can agencies white-label this service for their clients?

Yes — our white-label program includes white-label reporting so backups appear under your own brand.

19\. What if I'm on shared hosting — does that change anything?

Shared hosting environments benefit even more from off-site backup, since resource limits and neighbor site issues are more common there.

20\. Do you support WooCommerce and e-commerce backups?

Yes, including database heavy stores where order and inventory data changes frequently.

21\. What happens during the free backup audit?

We review your current backup setup (if any), check for gaps, and give you an honest recommendation — no obligation to sign up.

22\. How do I get started?

Start with the free backup audit above, or contact us directly — setup typically completes within 1–2 business days.
