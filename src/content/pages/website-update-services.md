## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about website update services, staging testing, update costs, and SLAs:

1\. What is a website update service?

A website update service is a managed program where a professional team handles all software updates for your website — including WordPress core, plugins, themes, and PHP versions — on a scheduled, recurring basis. It typically includes pre-update backups, testing, and post-update quality checks.

2\. How often should a website be updated?

WordPress plugins release updates continuously, often several times per week. Security patches should be applied within 24-48 hours of release. WordPress core minor updates can be applied as they are released; major versions benefit from a few days of community testing first. With a monthly maintenance plan, all of this is handled automatically.

3\. How much do website updates cost?

Professional website update services typically range from $49 to $299 per month depending on site complexity and the level of testing required. Our plans start at $49/month for simple sites and go up to $299/month for eCommerce and high-traffic sites. One-time update services are also available for sites that only need occasional attention.

4\. Can a website update break my site?

Yes — particularly major WordPress core updates and certain plugin updates that affect database structure or template compatibility. This is why professional maintenance includes a pre-update backup and, on higher plans, staging environment testing. If an update causes a problem, we restore your site and investigate the root cause before reattempting.

5\. Do you work with all types of websites or just WordPress?

Our primary specialization is WordPress websites, which power over 40% of all websites on the internet. WordPress sites have specific, recurring update needs that our process is built around. If you have a non-WordPress site, contact us — we work with other CMS platforms on a case-by-case basis.

6\. How long does a website update take?

For most websites, a standard monthly update cycle — including backup, all software updates, and post-update testing — takes 1-3 hours of professional time. You do not see any of this. For sites requiring staging environment testing, the cycle may take up to one business day. Updates are typically completed during off-peak hours.

7\. What happens if an update breaks something?

We restore your site from the pre-update backup immediately. Your site will be back online in the same state it was in before the update. We then investigate the root cause — usually a plugin conflict or compatibility issue — resolve it, and reattempt the update in a safe sequence.

8\. Do I need to be available when updates are happening?

No. Updates happen in the background on a schedule. You will receive a report afterward showing what was done. If we find anything that requires your input — for example, a plugin that needs to be replaced — we will email you with a specific, clear recommendation.

9\. What is included in the monthly maintenance report?

Your monthly report includes: a list of all software updated, before/after version numbers, any issues found and how they were resolved, your site's current performance score, security scan results, and backup confirmation. It is written in plain English — no technical jargon.

10\. Do you update websites outside of WordPress?

Our core service is built around WordPress. For Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, or custom-built sites, the update requirements differ significantly. Contact us to discuss your specific situation and we will advise honestly on whether we can help.

11\. Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

All of our monthly plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We do not believe in locking clients into contracts — we prefer to earn your business month after month.

12\. What is the difference between website maintenance and website hosting?

Hosting provides the server infrastructure your website runs on — disk space, bandwidth, and computing resources. Maintenance is the ongoing care of your website's software, security, content, and performance. Most hosting providers offer automated backups but do not handle plugin or theme updates, security patching, or QA testing.

13\. Should I hire a freelancer or a maintenance agency for website updates?

Both can work. A freelancer is a person — availability depends on their schedule, and if they are sick or on vacation, your site may go unserviced. A maintenance agency has processes, redundancy, and documented procedures. We combine the personal attention of a freelancer with the reliability of an agency operation.

14\. How do you handle security if my site gets hacked?

Security incident response is available as an add-on service. If your site is already compromised, contact us for an emergency cleanup quote. Prevention — through regular updates and security scanning — is far more cost-effective than remediation after the fact.

15\. Do you work with WooCommerce websites?

Yes. WooCommerce websites have additional update complexity because payment gateway plugins, WooCommerce extensions, and the WooCommerce core itself all update independently. Our Agency plan is specifically designed for WooCommerce stores and includes checkout testing after every update.

16\. What is a staging environment and do I need one?

A staging environment is a private copy of your live website where updates are tested before being applied to the real site. It is essential for eCommerce sites, membership sites, and any website where downtime or broken functionality would have immediate financial consequences. Staging testing is included in our Professional and Agency plans.

17\. How do website updates affect my SEO?

Done correctly, regular updates improve SEO by maintaining site speed, security (HTTPS), and technical health — all of which are factors in Google's ranking systems. Poorly handled updates can temporarily affect rankings if they cause downtime or break important site structure. Our process minimizes this risk.

18\. What if I use a page builder like Elementor or Divi?

Page builders like Elementor add an additional layer of update complexity because they have their own update cycles and compatibility requirements with plugins and themes. We have significant experience with Elementor specifically and include page builder compatibility checks in our update process.

19\. Can you update my website if I do not know my login credentials?

We can help you recover access. During onboarding, we will need WordPress admin access and for some tasks, hosting control panel access. We will guide you through the process of providing secure access and can assist with credential recovery if needed.

20\. Do you offer white-label maintenance for web agencies?

Yes. Our agency plan supports white-label delivery — we handle the technical maintenance and reporting, and you deliver it to your clients under your own brand. This is how many web design agencies offer professional maintenance without building an internal operations team. Contact us for agency volume pricing.

21\. What makes your service different from a cheap $19/month maintenance plugin?

Automated maintenance tools can apply updates — but they cannot test what breaks afterward, restore a site when something goes wrong, or make judgments about update sequencing. A human expert reviews changelogs, identifies risk, tests functionality, and makes the call. Tools do not catch that a plugin update conflicts with your specific theme configuration. People do.

22\. Do you offer one-time website updates instead of monthly plans?

Yes. If your site has not been updated in a long time and you need a comprehensive catch-up, we offer a one-time update audit service. We will update everything, test functionality, and provide a full report. However, we always recommend transitioning to a monthly plan — because updates do not stop.
