## Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, expert answers to common questions about website redesigns, SEO preservation, URL redirects, pricing, and project timelines.

### Will redesigning my website hurt my SEO rankings?

Not when executed with an SEO-first approach. While traditional design agencies often break URLs and cause a 30%-70% traffic drop, our process uses 1:1 301 redirect mapping, metadata retention, and staging audits to protect of your Google rankings.

### How do you redesign a website without losing SEO?

We crawl your existing site to map every URL, preserve top-ranking H1-H3 heading structures and metadata patterns, test the new build on an isolated staging server, and submit updated XML sitemaps to Search Console upon launch.

### How long does a website redesign take?

On average, a custom website redesign takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on page volume, custom CMS features, and content complexity. Read our [full guide on redesign timelines](/blogs/).

### How much does a website redesign cost?

Pricing ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for small business sites, $2,500 to $5,500 for growing eCommerce brands, and custom estimates for enterprise portals. We provide transparent fixed-rate quotes tailored to your scope.

### What's included in a website redesign SEO checklist?

Key steps include a full pre-launch URL crawl, 1:1 301 redirect mapping, content equity preservation, staging QA testing, Core Web Vitals speed tuning, and 30–60 days of post-launch rank monitoring.

### Do you handle 301 redirects during a redesign?

Yes, 100%. We map every single legacy URL to its corresponding new destination before the new site goes live, ensuring zero broken 404 links and preserving your backlink authority.

### How often should a business redesign its website?

Most businesses redesign their site every 2 to 4 years to keep pace with changing mobile standards, Google Core Web Vitals algorithms, brand evolution, and conversion design benchmarks.

### Can you redesign my site without changing my CMS?

Yes. If you love your current CMS (e.g., WordPress or Shopify), we can completely overhaul your front-end UI/UX and theme code without disturbing your underlying CMS content or database setup.

### Do you offer website redesign services for ecommerce sites?

Yes. We specialize in WooCommerce and Shopify store redesigns, preserving high-value product category URLs, customer order histories, and SKU equity while optimizing checkout conversion rates.

### Will my content be preserved during the redesign?

Yes. All existing blog posts, core service copy, image assets, and customer data are safely backed up and migrated to the new design framework without content loss.

### What happens to my backlinks after a redesign?

By establishing accurate 301 redirects from old URLs to new pages, of your incoming backlink equity and domain authority is transferred seamlessly to the new site.

### Do you provide post-launch SEO monitoring?

Yes. We monitor organic rank tracking and Search Console crawl reports for 30 to 60 days post-launch to catch and fix minor index fluctuations immediately.

### Can you redesign a WordPress site without breaking plugins?

Yes. We conduct a thorough PHP compatibility audit on all active plugins on a staging server first, replacing outdated or slow plugins with lightweight, secure alternatives.

### What's the difference between a redesign and a refresh?

A website refresh updates minor visual elements like fonts, colors, or banners. A full redesign re-engineers the site structure, code framework, mobile UX, and information architecture for higher conversions.

### Do you work with businesses outside the US?

Yes! We serve growing businesses across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Netherlands, France, Spain, Brazil, Ireland, and Switzerland.

### Do I need a full redesign or just a partial update?

During our initial free audit, we analyze your current site performance. If your backend code and speed are solid, we may recommend a targeted UI/UX overhaul rather than an expensive full rebuild.

### How do you handle mobile responsiveness in a redesign?

We follow a mobile-first design strategy. Every wireframe is optimized for mobile touch targets, fast loading times, and responsive layouts before scaling up to desktop screens.

### Will I be involved in the design process?

Absolutely. You review and approve wireframes, design prototypes, and staging builds at key milestones before we move forward to live deployment.

### What ongoing support is available after launch?

We offer dedicated [ongoing website maintenance plans](/monthly-website-maintenance-packages/) covering daily backups, security monitoring, speed tuning, and monthly performance reports.
