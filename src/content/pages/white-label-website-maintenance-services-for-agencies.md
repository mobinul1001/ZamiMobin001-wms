Definitional Overview

## What Is White Label Website Maintenance?

### Definition: White Label Website Maintenance

**White label website maintenance** is a B2B reseller model where a specialized technical team manages, secures, updates, and optimizes client websites on behalf of a digital agency. All client reports, support tickets, and communication are delivered under the agency’s brand name, keeping the backend team completely invisible.

### How the Agency Reseller Model Works

In a **private label web support** partnership, your agency owns the client relationship, sets retail pricing, and sends unbranded monthly reports. Meanwhile, our technical team executes 24/7 security scans, core/plugin updates, cloud backups, and small site edits behind the scenes.

Whether managing single business sites or complex multi-site client portfolios, our team delivers [our full WordPress maintenance service](/wordpress-website-maintenance-packages/) with strict uptime SLAs and strict non-disclosure protection.

### What is white label website maintenance?

White label website maintenance is an outsourced B2B reseller service where our technical team manages, updates, and secures your clients' websites under your agency's brand name. All client communication and monthly reports carry your logo, keeping our backend team completely invisible.

### How does white label WordPress maintenance work for agencies?

You onboard your client sites to our agency portal. Our engineers execute 24/7 security scans, daily backups, staging-first updates, and small edits. You receive unbranded PDF reports each month that you forward directly to your clients.

### Will my clients know you're maintaining their site?

No. We operate 100% white label. We never contact your clients directly unless requested. All email ticket responses, plugin branding, and reporting dashboards display your agency's name, logo, and brand colors.

### What's included in a white label website maintenance plan?

Plans include core, theme, and plugin updates, 24/7 security WAF firewalls, daily offsite backups, PageSpeed tuning, unlimited small edits, and unbranded monthly client reports. Explore our full [WordPress maintenance plans](/wordpress-website-maintenance-packages/).

Yes. WordPress is our primary core competency. Our engineers are specialists in WordPress core, WooCommerce, Yoast, Elementor, Divi, and custom PHP theme frameworks.

### How much does a white label website maintenance retainer cost?

Our wholesale agency retainers start at $59/site/month for 1-5 sites, dropping to $39/site/month for 6-20 sites. Custom volume pricing is available for agencies managing 20+ client sites.

### Is there a minimum number of sites required to become a partner?

No minimum required! Whether you are a solo freelancer with 1 client site or a scaling digital agency with 100+ sites, you qualify for our white-label partner program.

### Can I get monthly white label services without a long-term contract?

Yes. All our agency partner plans operate on a flexible month-to-month retainer with zero long-term lock-in contracts. You can add, upgrade, or remove client sites at any time.

### How fast do you respond to emergency support requests?

We guarantee an average 4-hour SLA response time for standard tickets, and a 1-hour priority emergency response SLA for critical server outages or security incidents.

### Do you provide white label client reporting under our branding?

Yes. Every monthly report PDF is generated with your agency logo, brand colors, contact information, and unbranded performance metrics ready to forward to your clients.

### What platforms do you support besides WordPress?

In addition to WordPress and WooCommerce, our technical team supports Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, and custom PHP headless web applications.

Yes. We offer fully white-labeled WordPress care plans that agencies can mark up and resell to their clients at 50%+ profit margins.

We perform automated daily offsite cloud backups via our [website backup service](/wordpress-backup-services/) and active WAF firewall monitoring via our [website security services](/wordpress-malware-removal-service/).

### What happens if a client's site gets hacked?

If an infection occurs, our engineers execute immediate [malware removal](/wordpress-malware-removal/) and [hack recovery](/website-hacked-recovery-services/) free of charge as part of your maintenance plan guarantee.
