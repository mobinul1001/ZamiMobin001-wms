Clarifications

## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Website Maintenance Services.

#### Do I need to be a developer to become a partner?

No. The program is designed specifically for professionals and agencies who manage client relationships or business opportunities but do not want to execute technical server configurations, bug fixes, or plugin updates themselves.

#### Can I set my own price for my clients?

Yes. Under the reseller model, your client-facing retail price is completely determined by you according to your business model, service bundles, and agreement with WMS.

#### Can I keep the difference between my client price and WMS price?

Yes. Under an applicable reseller arrangement, the spread between what you invoice your client and the wholesale rate you pay WMS represents your gross margin, after accounting for your local payment gateway fees and taxes.

#### Does WMS charge my client directly?

Not necessarily. In a reseller arrangement, your client pays you directly, and you pay WMS our agreed partner fee. Direct client invoicing only occurs under dedicated referral agreements if explicitly requested.

#### Can I sell WMS services under my own brand?

Yes, white-label arrangements are available to eligible agency partners subject to minimum volume qualifications and an approved commercial agreement.

#### What if I only have one client right now?

You can still apply! We evaluate single-client situations and help determine whether a reseller tier or a referral introduction is the best commercial fit for you.

#### What types of websites can you maintain?

Our core focus is WordPress and WooCommerce ecosystems, alongside custom PHP, HTML/CSS, Shopify, and standard corporate CMS setups. Specific frameworks are verified during initial review.

#### Do I get paid for simply submitting a lead?

Not under the standard reseller model. The standard model is based on purchasing WMS services at an agreed Partner Price and setting your own margin. Separate affiliate or referral contracts can be established upon request.

#### Important Partner Terms

• The WMS Partner Program is a business-to-business partnership opportunity and does not guarantee income, clients, projects, or profits.

• Partner pricing, service scope, payment terms, client responsibilities, branding rights, and conditions are explicitly confirmed before service begins.

• Partners remain solely responsible for their own client agreements, pricing structures, invoicing, taxes, business expenses, and legal obligations.

• WMS reserves the right to review, approve, reject, suspend, or terminate partner applications in accordance with our partner governance agreement.
