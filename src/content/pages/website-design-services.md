Educational Definition

## What Is Website Design?

### Definition: Website Design

**Website design** is the process of planning, conceptualizing, and arranging content, layout, and visual elements for the web. It combines user interface (UI) design, user experience (UX) research, color theory, typography, and information architecture to create an aesthetically pleasing, mobile-first, and easy-to-navigate digital experience.

<div class="cards">

<div class="card">

### Design vs. Development — What's the Difference?

While often used interchangeably, **web design** and web development are two distinct disciplines. Website design focuses on the front-end visual look, feel, wireframes, color psychology, and conversion user journeys.

Web development, on the other hand, deals with the underlying code (HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, PHP), CMS database integration, server logic, and technical performance that brings the visual design to life. Our team seamlessly combines both disciplines under one roof.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Website Design

Find clear, direct answers to common questions about custom web design pricing, project timelines, CMS platforms, SEO, and post-launch support.

### How much does professional website design cost?

Professional website design packages start at $1,499 for small business 5-page sites, $2,999 for growth businesses up to 10 pages, and custom quotes for enterprise portals.

### What's included in a website design package?

Our packages include custom UI/UX design, mobile responsiveness, SEO architecture, Schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, and 30 to 90 days of post-launch support.

### How long does it take to design a website?

On average, a custom website design project takes 4 to 8 weeks from discovery to live launch, depending on page count and custom functionality.

### Do you offer website design for small businesses?

Yes. We offer budget-friendly small business website design packages engineered to establish local search authority and generate customer phone calls.

### What's the difference between website design and website development?

Website design handles the front-end visual look, UI/UX wireframes, and layout. Web development deals with underlying code, CMS integration, and backend functionality.

### Do you build custom websites or use templates?

We build custom websites from scratch using Figma wireframes and clean code. We never use pre-made, bloated drag-and-drop templates.

### Will my new website be mobile-friendly and responsive?

Yes. Every website we build follows a mobile-first design philosophy, rigorously tested across 15+ mobile, tablet, and desktop screen sizes before launch.

### Is SEO included in your website design services?

Yes. Every site includes foundational SEO architecture: clean heading tags, Schema markup, PageSpeed optimization, and XML sitemaps built-in from day one.

### Which platforms do you design on — WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace?

We design custom sites on WordPress (Gutenberg/WooCommerce), Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, and custom headless PHP/React frameworks.

### Do I own my website after it's built?

Yes, 100%. Upon final project completion, you own full intellectual property rights, files, database, and domain ownership with no lock-in clauses.

### What happens after my website launches — is maintenance included?

Every package includes 30 to 90 days of post-launch care. You can also pair your new build with our [monthly website maintenance packages](/monthly-website-maintenance-packages/).

### Can you redesign my existing website instead of building a new one?

Yes. If you want to overhaul an existing site without losing SEO rankings, explore our dedicated [website redesign services](/website-redesign-services/).

### Do you design websites for specific industries like law firms or healthcare?

Yes. We build tailored websites for law firms, medical clinics, contractors, eCommerce brands, SaaS companies, and professional services firms.

### How many revisions are included in the design process?

Starter plans include 1 round, Growth plans include 2 rounds, and Enterprise packages include unlimited wireframe/prototype revisions within scope.

### Will my website be secure and protected from hackers?

Yes. We deploy SSL HTTPS encryption, file permission hardening, and WAF firewall rules. Learn more about our [website security services](/wordpress-malware-removal-service/).

### Do you offer website design packages for agencies (white-label)?

Yes! We partner with digital agencies to deliver unbranded web design and maintenance. Explore our [white-label maintenance solutions](/white-label-website-maintenance-services-for-agencies/).

### What information do you need from me to start my website design?

We need your brand assets (logo, colors), general copy or content inventory, competitor examples, and domain/hosting login details.

### Can you migrate my content from my old website to the new one?

Yes. We handle full database, blog post, image, and page content migrations with 1:1 301 redirect mapping to protect your Google rankings.

### Do you offer payment plans for website design projects?

Yes. Most projects are split into a 50% deposit to initiate design and 50% upon final client signoff prior to live server deployment.

### What if I need urgent changes or support after launch?

Every design comes with dedicated post-launch support. You can also submit emergency requests directly to our team anytime.
