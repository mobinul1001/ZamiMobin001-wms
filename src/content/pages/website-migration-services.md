## What Is Our Website Migration Service?

A **website migration service** is the process of safely transferring a website’s files, database, DNS records, and SSL certificates from one web host, domain name, or CMS platform to another. Whether you are upgrading servers or rebranding your domain, professional migration ensures your site moves smoothly without losing traffic, data, or Google search rankings.

While DIY plugins exist, they frequently cause timeout errors, database corruption, or broken 301 redirects on complex sites. Our dedicated **site migration service** eliminates these risks by testing everything on a secure staging server before going live — supported by our ongoing [WordPress maintenance](/wordpress-website-maintenance-packages/) and [website security](/wordpress-malware-removal-service/) standards.

### Host & Server Migration

Moving your website from slow or unreliable web hosting to a fast VPS, dedicated server, or cloud host (like WP Engine, Kinsta, or SiteGround) with zero file or database loss.

Includes DB export, SSL & PHP tuning

### Platform & CMS Migration

Transferring your site from closed builders like Wix, Squarespace, or Shopify to WordPress/WooCommerce (or vice versa) while keeping all pages, content, blog posts, and media safe.

## Website Migration Services — Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, honest answers to the most common questions about migrating your website, server transfers, SEO preservation, and data safety.

### How long does website migration take?

Most standard website migrations are completed within 4 to 8 hours. We first move and test your entire site on a staging environment before performing the final DNS cutover. Express 24-hour turnaround is available for urgent transfers.

### Will my SEO rankings be affected by migration?

No. We follow strict SEO-safe protocols. We map all existing URLs, configure exact 301 redirects, preserve canonical tags, and update Google Search Console to ensure your search rankings and organic traffic remain 100% intact.

### How much downtime will my site experience?

We guarantee under 15 minutes of downtime — and in most cases, zero downtime! Because we prepare and test everything on a staging server first, visitors won't notice any interruption during the DNS cutover.

### How much does website migration cost?

Our website migration service pricing starts at a flat $99 for basic sites and $199 for standard WordPress websites up to 5GB. Enterprise, multisite, or large eCommerce stores receive custom fixed-rate quotes with zero hidden fees.

### What platforms and CMS do you support?

We support transfers across all major web platforms, including WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, Joomla, Drupal, Webflow, HTML/static sites, and custom PHP databases.

### Is my website data and credentials safe?

Yes, absolutely. All credentials, database files, and host logins are encrypted during transfer. We follow strict confidentiality policies and are happy to sign an NDA upon request before starting work.

### Do you offer a guarantee if something breaks?

Yes. Every migration includes a 30-day post-migration warranty. If any error or broken link occurs post-launch, we fix it immediately free of charge. In case of previous failed DIY attempts, explore our [emergency website recovery](/website-repair-services/) service.

### Can you migrate a large eCommerce store?

Yes. We specialize in WooCommerce and Shopify store migrations. We safely transfer all product catalogs, SKUs, customer order histories, user accounts, and payment gateway configurations with zero data loss.

### Do I need to be available during the migration?

No. Once you provide the necessary login details, our migration engineers handle everything asynchronously. We keep you updated via email/WhatsApp at each major stage of the process.

### What happens to my business email during migration?

We carefully preserve all MX, SPF, and DKIM DNS records during the NameServer update. Your business emails continue sending and receiving without any interruption or dropped messages.
