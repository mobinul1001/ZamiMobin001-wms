Get all three right and your site becomes your best salesperson. Miss one and you quietly bleed rides every day.

<a id="why"></a>

## The StakesYour Website Decides the Booking — Before You Meet the Customer

Caribbean transport is fundamentally different from a mainland cab company. Most of your customers are tourists who have never been to your island, don't know the local operators, and are planning from their phone days or weeks before they arrive. They don't ask a neighbor for a recommendation — they search Google, tap the first result that looks trustworthy, and book.

That means the operator with the better website wins — even against a competitor with better drivers, better boats, and lower prices. Your website is your storefront, your sales pitch, and your booking desk all at once, and it never sleeps. According to [travel research from Google](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/consumer-insights/consumer-trends/travel-trends-tourism-statistics/), the overwhelming majority of travelers research and book trips online, mostly on mobile — which is exactly the moment your site has to show up and convert.

### You get about 3 seconds

A tourist deciding who to trust with their airport pickup judges your credibility almost instantly. A **fast, modern, mobile-friendly site** earns that trust; a slow or dated one sends them back to the search results — and to your competitor. Everything below exists to win those 3 seconds and the booking that follows.

<a id="features"></a>

## The EssentialsWhat Your Transport Website Must Have

A transport website has one job: turn a searching tourist into a confirmed ride. These are the elements that actually do that — the non-negotiables for any serious operator.

**Mobile-first design.** The vast majority of your visitors are on a phone. Your site must be effortless to read, tap, and book on a small screen — not a shrunken desktop layout. If it's awkward on mobile, you lose most of your customers before they try.

**An obvious way to book or inquire.** A clear "Book Now" or "Get a Quote" button on every screen. Never make a tourist hunt for how to reach you — every extra step loses bookings.

**Online booking or an inquiry form.** Capture the pickup location, date, time, and party size automatically, so customers can request a ride at any hour and you wake up to real, ready-to-confirm leads — not missed calls.

**Clear routes, rates, and schedules.** Tourists want to know what a ride costs and when it runs *before* they commit. Transparent pricing removes hesitation and cuts endless back-and-forth messaging.

**WhatsApp and one-tap contact.** Many island travelers prefer WhatsApp. One-tap WhatsApp, call, and message buttons capture the bookers who want to reach a human directly.

**Trust signals.** Real customer reviews, photos of your vehicles or boats, licensing and insurance details, and a clear "about us" — everything a nervous first-time visitor needs to hand over their money and their arrival plans.

**Fast loading speed.** Tourists on hotel Wi-Fi, cruise signal, or roaming data abandon slow sites instantly. Optimized images and clean code keep visitors from bouncing — and help you rank on Google.

**Local SEO foundations.** Proper page titles, your island in the right places, a Google Business Profile, and structured data so search engines know exactly what you do and where. This is what gets you found in the first place.

**Secure (HTTPS) and easy to update.** An SSL padlock so browsers don't flag you as "Not Secure," and a site you can actually update — seasonal rates, new routes, holiday schedules — without hiring a developer every time.

## Take the BookingBooking Tools & Software Worth Knowing

"How do I actually take bookings online?" is the question every operator asks. The good news: you don't need to build a booking system from scratch. There are proven tools that plug into your website, take payments, manage availability, and send confirmations automatically. The right one depends on your size and how you sell.

Simple inquiry form + WhatsApp

The easiest start: a form that emails you booking requests, plus one-tap WhatsApp. Perfect for smaller operators who confirm each ride personally. Low cost, no monthly fee.

FareHarbor

A popular tours-and-activities booking platform used across the Caribbean for tours, transfers, and excursions — handles availability, payments, and confirmations.

Bokun (by Tripadvisor)

Booking engine plus distribution — lets you sell directly and also list on channels like Viator and Tripadvisor to reach more tourists.

Rezdy

Tour and activity booking software with strong channel-manager features for operators who want resellers and agents to book their seats.

Checkfront / Peek Pro

Flexible reservation systems suited to transfers, rentals, and tours, with online payments and calendar management.

TryBooking / WooCommerce Bookings

If your site runs on WordPress, a bookings plugin can turn it into a full reservation system you fully own — no per-booking commission to a third party.

### A word on commissions

Third-party platforms are great for reach, but many take a **commission on every booking**. A smart setup uses your own website and booking system as the commission-free "home base," and channels like Viator or Tripadvisor as extra reach — so you're never fully dependent on a platform that keeps a cut. Owning your booking flow is how you keep more of every fare. Not sure which fits your business? [Talk to our team](/contact/) and we'll match the tool to how you sell.

## Get FoundWhy SEO Isn't Optional for Transport Businesses

You can have the most beautiful, booking-ready website on the island — but if it doesn't appear when a tourist searches "\[your island\] airport taxi" or "water taxi \[your island\]," it may as well not exist. That's what SEO (search engine optimization) solves: it's the work that makes Google show your site to the exact people already looking for a ride.

For Caribbean transport, a few things matter most. **Local SEO** ties your business to your island and routes, so you show up in local searches and on Google Maps. **A Google Business Profile** — with photos, reviews, and accurate hours — often appears above the regular results and drives a huge share of calls and bookings. **On-page SEO** (clear titles, descriptions, and content about your specific routes and services) tells Google exactly what you offer. And **reviews** build the trust that both Google and tourists reward.

SEO isn't a one-time task, either — it compounds. Every month your site stays fast, fresh, and well-maintained, it climbs and holds its rankings, bringing in free, repeat bookings without ad spend. That's why SEO and maintenance are two sides of the same coin. ## Buyer BewareHow Typical Agencies Hold Transport Businesses Hostage

This is the part of the industry nobody tells you about — and it's why so many transport operators feel trapped with a website they're not happy with. Many web companies quietly build their business model around locking you in, so that leaving becomes painful or impossible. Watch for these tactics:

**They own your domain name.** Some agencies register your domain in *their* account instead of yours. If you try to leave, you can lose the web address your customers and Google already know — a devastating hit to a business that runs on being found.

**They lock you into proprietary platforms.** Your site is built on a closed system only they can edit. You can't move it, you can't hire anyone else, and you can't take your content with you — so you're stuck paying whatever they charge.

**They hold your hosting and logins.** No admin access, no hosting credentials, no way to touch your own site. Every tiny change — a new rate, a holiday schedule — means paying them and waiting.

**They charge for every small edit.** Because you can't update anything yourself, you're billed for changes that should take minutes, turning a simple site into an endless invoice.

**They keep your booking data and reviews locked in.** Your customer list, booking history, and reviews live in their system, so leaving means starting from zero — a powerful way to keep you paying.

**Cancel, and the site disappears.** Stop paying and the whole site vanishes overnight, because you never actually owned it — you were only renting it, without being told.

None of this is necessary, and none of it is in your interest. A fair provider builds you a site *you* own, on a standard platform (like WordPress), with your domain in your name and full access in your hands — and earns your business every month with good work, not by holding it hostage.

<a id="own"></a>

## Protect YourselfWhat You Should Always Own and Control

Before you hire anyone — us or anyone else — make sure any website deal gives you these. If a provider won't, that's your warning sign:

-   **Your domain name, registered in your name.** It's your business identity. It should be in an account you control, always.
-   **Full admin access to your website.** You should be able to log in, see everything, and hand it to another provider if you ever choose to.
-   **A standard, portable platform.** Something like WordPress that any professional can work on — not a locked proprietary system only one company can touch.
-   **Your hosting account and credentials.** You (or an account clearly in your name) should own the hosting your site lives on.
-   **Your content, bookings, and reviews.** Your photos, text, customer data, and reviews are yours to export and keep.
-   **Clear, no-lock-in terms.** If you ever leave, you keep everything and the site keeps running. Good providers are happy to put this in writing.
