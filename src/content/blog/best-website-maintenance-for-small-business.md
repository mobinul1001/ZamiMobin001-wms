## Section 06What Affects Website Maintenance Pricing?

Two small business websites can have very different maintenance costs. These are the factors that move the price:

-   **Platform & complexity** — a simple brochure site costs less than a site with custom features or WooCommerce.
-   **Number of plugins** — more plugins means more updates and compatibility testing.
-   **Update & backup frequency** — weekly vs daily care changes the price.
-   **Security level** — malware removal and firewalls add cost but prevent expensive breaches.
-   **Content edits & support** — included edit hours and priority support raise the tier.
