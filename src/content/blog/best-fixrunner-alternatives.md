## The BaselineWhat FixRunner Offers

Let's give credit first: FixRunner earned its reputation. It's a WordPress maintenance and support service with more than a decade in the business, a widely cited one-hour response commitment, and a publicly displayed satisfaction rate near 99% across 150,000+ resolved tickets. Its reported 2026 plans start around **$59/month** (annual, with about 90 minutes of support time), rising through **Rocket** (~$89/month), **Advanced** (~$149/month), and enterprise tiers above that. All plans include core, theme, and plugin updates, malware removal, daily backups, uptime monitoring, security scans, and even speed optimization on the cheapest plan — plus a support-ticket system for on-demand fixes.

For owners who mainly want fast, ad-hoc technical help, that's a genuinely good offering. But "fast fixes" isn't the same as "best overall value," and a few structural details send many owners looking for something different.

<a id="why"></a>

## The GapsWhy People Look for a FixRunner Alternative

These aren't attacks on FixRunner — they're the practical reasons real site owners shop around. If any of these matter to you, an alternative may fit better:

**Hosting isn't included.** FixRunner maintains your site but hosting is an optional extra (via third parties like WP Engine or SiteGround), so you're still paying a separate hosting bill on top — often $30–$100/month elsewhere. That raises your true monthly cost above the sticker price.

**A minimum contract, not cancel-anytime.** FixRunner plans carry a reported three-month minimum subscription period, so you're committed upfront rather than free to leave whenever you like.

**Support is metered by time.** Plans include a fixed block of support time (around 90 minutes on the entry plan). Go over it and you pay more — which can get unpredictable on a site that needs frequent work.

**Price transparency is limited.** Several industry reviews note that FixRunner's full pricing isn't clearly published up front, so you may need to request a quote to see the real number for your situation.

**Fix-focused, not full-service.** FixRunner shines at reactive fixes and support tickets, but it doesn't bundle things like hosting, content, and SEO into one roof the way a full-service partner does — leaving you to coordinate multiple vendors.

**You may want stronger third-party proof.** FixRunner publishes its own satisfaction numbers, but some owners prefer a provider whose rating is independently verifiable across multiple public review platforms.

<a id="lookfor"></a>

## ChecklistWhat to Look For in a FixRunner Alternative

Whatever provider you choose, judge it on these criteria — they separate a real maintenance partner from a metered support line:

**Hosting included, or the true cost shown.** A plan that bundles hosting (or includes it free for a period) lowers your real monthly cost versus a "cheap" plan that leaves hosting on you.

**Flexible, cancel-anytime terms.** Look for no long-term minimum contract, so you stay because the service is worth it — not because you're locked in.

**Transparent published pricing.** You should see exactly what a plan costs and includes without having to chase a quote.

**Staging-tested updates.** Updates tested on a staging copy before going live, so a routine update can't take your site down.

**Genuine security with fast cleanup.** Firewall, malware scanning, and quick removal if something slips through. (If you've been hit, see our guide to [WordPress malware removal](/wordpress-malware-removal/).)

**Real, human emergency support.** A clear response time and an emergency channel like WhatsApp for when your site goes down at the worst moment.

**Independently verifiable reputation.** A rating you can confirm yourself across public review platforms — not just self-reported numbers.
