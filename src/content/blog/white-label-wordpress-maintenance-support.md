## What Is White Label WordPress Maintenance?

White label WordPress maintenance (also called white label WP maintenance, white-label WP care, or reseller WordPress support) is a service where a specialist provider performs all WordPress maintenance work for your clients, delivered entirely under *your* brand. Your clients see your logo, your plan names, and your reports — never the provider's.

In practice, you sell a WordPress care plan to your client, we do the maintenance (core, plugin and theme updates, security, backups, speed, and support) at a wholesale per-site rate, and you keep the difference between your retail price and our wholesale rate. It is the fastest way for an agency to add recurring revenue without building an in-house maintenance team.

For context on what maintenance involves and typical retail rates, see our guides on [WordPress maintenance cost](/how-much-does-wordpress-website-maintenance-cost/) and the full [WordPress maintenance packages](/wordpress-website-maintenance-packages/) we deliver.
