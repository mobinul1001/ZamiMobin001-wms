### Against the main plans
Lower than retail is fine under the owner's rule ($59 and $39 against Essential at $79), but these points still contradict or confuse:
1. **Plan name:** "Growth Plan" here has the same name as the main Growth plan at $149. Proposed names: "Agency Starter" and "Agency Growth".
2. **More for less:** at $39 per site the page lists "Unlimited Small Edits (30m/ea)", staging testing and a dedicated account manager. Main Business at $299 has 6 hours of edits and staging-tested updates. The owner must decide whether these wholesale features stay as written.
3. **Who can buy:** "1–5 client websites" lets one site owner buy at $59 instead of Essential at $79. The page should say agencies only.
4. **Margin claim:** the page promises "50%+ profit margins". At the $79 retail price, $39 gives about 50.6% but $59 gives only about 25%. The owner must confirm or remove this claim.
